================================================================================
Gaia DR3 star positions, shipped as gaia-dr3-g11.vcat (CC BY-NC 3.0 IGO)
================================================================================

gaia-dr3-g11.vcat holds star positions, G magnitudes and BP-RP colours from Gaia
Data Release 3, down to G = 11. Valerian reads it at run time to solve the position
of an image (valerian solve) and to calibrate colour (valerian photocal); it is
data, not part of the program's code.
