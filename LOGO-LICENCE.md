## What is covered

Everything under `assets/branding/`:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `valerian-logo.png` | The original artwork, unmodified. |
| `icons/valerian-*.png`, `valerian.ico`, `valerian.icns` | The same artwork resized, unchanged otherwise, for the application icon, the installers and the packages. |

And two derived uses, which stay with the logo:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `docs/book/src/images/valerian-logo.png` | The galaxy part of the artwork, cropped and resized, for the header of the manual. |
| The top bar of `valerian-gui` | The same crop, drawn from the embedded 128 pixel icon, which also appears in the screenshots in the manual (`docs/book/src/images/ui/`). |

The original is identified by its SHA-256:

```text

```

## What you may do

* Use the logo, **unmodified**, in unmodified builds of Valerian, such as the
  releases published by the author.
* Use the unmodified logo to refer to the Valerian project: an article, a
  review, a link to the project page.

Anything else needs the author's written permission.

## Forks and modified versions

If you distribute a modified version of Valerian, or a fork, you must **replace
the logo and every image derived from it** with your own, so that your version
is not mistaken for the original. This goes with
[ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md](ADDITIONAL-TERMS.md) (GPLv3 section 7(c)): a modified
version must not be presented as the original work or as made or endorsed by
Laurence Horrocks-Barlow.
